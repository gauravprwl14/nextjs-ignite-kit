# The Future of Serverless

*Posted on December 9, 2025*

Serverless computing is evolving rapidly. In this article, we explore the trade-offs between cold starts and cost optimization in modern cloud architectures.

## Introduction

The promise of serverless has always been: **pay for what you use**. But as enterprise adoption grows, we are seeing new patterns emerge...

## Key Takeaways

1.  **Cold Starts aren't the enemy**: They are a design constraint.
2.  **Edge Compute**: Moving logic closer to the user.
3.  **Cost**: It's not always cheaper.

## Conclusion

Serverless is here to stay, but it requires a shift in mindset.

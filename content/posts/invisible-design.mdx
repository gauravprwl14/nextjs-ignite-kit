# The Art of Invisible Design

Great design is often invisible. When we use an application that just *works*, we rarely stop to admire the button radius or the specific shade of grey used in the border. We are too busy accomplishing our task.

## The Role of Spacing

Whitespace is not empty space; it is an active design element. It guides the eye, creates hierarchy, and gives the content room to breathe.

> "Perfection is achieved not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away." - Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

## Typography as Voice

Your choice of typeface is the tone of voice for your application. A serif font might whisper tradition and authority, while a geometric sans-serif speaks of modernity and precision.

### Code Snippet Example

```tsx
function InvisibleComponent() {
  return (
    <div className="opacity-0 hover:opacity-100 transition-opacity">
      Now you see me.
    </div>
  );
}
```
